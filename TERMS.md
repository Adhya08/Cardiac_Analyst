## 5. Algorithmic Bounds & Limitations

The deep convolutional neural network (`resnet1d_ptbxl_5class.onnx`) is trained on 100 Hz downsampled 10-second voltage recordings from the PTB-XL database. 
- Model accuracy and generalization are bounded by its training distribution and validation performance (Macro AUROC 0.9265, Macro F1 0.7520 on PTB-XL Fold 10).
- Signal quality artifacts, baseline wander, electrode detachment, muscle tremors, and pediatric or non-adult physiology may yield false positives or false negatives.
- The dual-mode report feature uses deterministic clinical templates and rule-based explanations; it does not constitute clinical consultation.
